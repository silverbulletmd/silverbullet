SilverBullet has been designed to be malleable software: software that its users can tweak and extend to their liking.

SilverBullet’s core is relatively small. Much of its functionality is built with a mix of [[Plugs]] (JavaScript bundles) and [[Space Lua]] scripts. On top of this you can write a few lines of code _as part of your notes_ to create a custom command, a slash command or page template, and your notes app quickly becomes a tool shaped to your personal or your organization’s needs.

While not as fundamental in this approach as editors like [Emacs](https://www.gnu.org/software/emacs/), it draws a lot of inspiration from it.
