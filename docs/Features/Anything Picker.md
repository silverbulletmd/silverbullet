The anything picker is the [[Page Picker]] opened on its **All** [[Page Picker#Segments|segment]]: every page and document, including [[Library Page|Library pages]] and pages hidden with [[Reference/Theming/Page Decorations]].

To open, run the command ${widgets.commandButton("Navigate: Anything Picker")} using this button or [[Command Palette]], or press `Tab` in any other picker until you reach **All**.
