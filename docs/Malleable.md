SilverBullet has been designed to be _malleable software_: software with a solid foundation that its users can tweak and extend to their liking.

SilverBullet’s core is relatively small. Much of its functionality is built with a mix of [[Plugs]] (JavaScript bundles) and [[Space Lua]] scripts. On top of it, you can write code _as part of your space_ to create a custom command, a slash command, page template, or a super fancy widget. As you do so, your knowledge management system quickly becomes a tool shaped to your personal or your team’s needs.
