A _knowledge management system_ is a place to collect what you (or your team) knows.

In SilverBullet your notes are kept as a [[Concepts/Space]] (folder) of [[Markdown]] [[Concepts/Page|Pages]] on disk. As you write, SilverBullet continuously indexes your content — [[Concepts/Page|pages]], [[Concepts/Link|links]], [[Concepts/Task|tasks]], list items and their [[Concepts/Attribute|attributes]] — into queryable [[Object|Objects]]. You can then slice and reshape that data with [[Space Lua/Integrated Query|Queries]] (SLIQ) and surface the results right inside your notes.

A space may hold one person’s notes, a team’s knowledge base, or a project’s living documentation — the mechanics are identical either way. SilverBullet grew out of the **personal knowledge management** (PKM) tradition, and that remains one of the shapes a space takes. See [[Concepts/Space#Who a space is for]].
