SilverBullet is a note-taking and knowledge management system you can reshape, whose data stays yours in plain files.

**Make it yours.** There is no one-size-fits-all for knowledge management, so SilverBullet is [[Malleable]]: a solid core that you extend with [[Space Lua]] scripts, [[Command|commands]], [[Page Template|templates]] and [[Space Style|styling]], right inside your own space. Read more at [Make it yours](https://silverbullet.md/malleable).

**Your files, forever.** Your content is a folder of [[Markdown]] files on disk, with [[Revisions]] kept in [[Git]]. If SilverBullet ever disappeared, you could still read and edit everything with any other tool. Read more at [Open formats](https://silverbullet.md/open-formats).

**Your data, your call.** You decide where your data lives: on your own machine with the [[Install/Desktop|desktop app]], or on a [[Self Hosted]] server, with [[Zero Tracking]] and no third party to trust. See [[Data Sovereignty]] and [[Local First]]. Read more at [Your data](https://silverbullet.md/your-data).
